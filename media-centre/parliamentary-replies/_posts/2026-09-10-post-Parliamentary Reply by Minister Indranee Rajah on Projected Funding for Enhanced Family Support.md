<p><strong>FIFTEENTH PARLIAMENT OF SINGAPORE</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>WRITTEN REPLY</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>THURSDAY, 10 September 2026</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Mr Liang Eng Hwa:</strong>
</p>
<p>To ask the Prime Minister and Minister for Finance what is the projected
funding needed for the enhanced family support measures and the childcare
leave announced in the recent National Day Rally.</p>
<p><strong>Ms Indranee Rajah (for the Prime Minister)</strong>:</p>
<p>The budget estimates for the enhanced family support measures and childcare
leave are not yet finalised as the implementation details of the announced
measures are still being worked out and other measures are also being studied
by the Marriage and Parenthood Reset Workgroup.</p>
