<p><strong>FIFTEENTH PARLIAMENT OF SINGAPORE</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>WRITTEN REPLY</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>THURSDAY, 10 September 2026</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Ms Yeo Wan Ling:</strong>
</p>
<p>To ask the Prime Minister and Minister for Finance (a) whether the Government
will consider extending the $10,000 Post-Secondary Education Account (PSEA)
top-up under the SG Child Support Package to Singapore Citizen students
turning 18 in 2026; and (b) whether transitional support can be provided
for this cohort, particularly second-year Junior College students entering
post-secondary education in 2027.</p>
<p><strong>Ms Indranee Rajah (for the Prime Minister)</strong>:</p>
<p>As announced at the National Day Rally, all Singapore Citizen children
will receive a $10,000 top-up to their Post-Secondary Education Account
(PSEA) in the year they turn 17, to help defray their higher education
costs. We opted to set the eligibility cut-off at age 17 as it broadly
coincides with the completion of secondary school and the transition towards
post-secondary education.</p>
<p>This will apply to Singaporean children turning 17 starting from 2026.
We hope that Members will understand that all policies require an eligibility
cut-off. Hence, no matter which date is chosen, it is inevitable that some
will narrowly miss the eligibility window, as would be the case for the
PSEA top-up.</p>
