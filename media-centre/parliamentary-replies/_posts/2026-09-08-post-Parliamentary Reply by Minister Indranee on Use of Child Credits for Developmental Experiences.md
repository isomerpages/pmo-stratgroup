<p><strong>FIFTEENTH PARLIAMENT OF SINGAPORE</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>WRITTEN REPLY</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>TUESDAY, 8 August 2026</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Mr David Hoe:</strong>
</p>
<p>To ask the Prime Minister and Minister for Finance while retaining the
flexibility of the annual Child Credits for families, whether the Government
will consider ways to encourage or incentivise their use for children’s
developmental experiences, such as sports, arts, hobbies and other interests,
including by building on the objectives and ecosystem developed under the
Ministry of Culture, Community and Youth's Curiosity Credits initiative.</p>
<p><strong>Ms Indranee Rajah (for the Prime Minister)</strong>:</p>
<p>As part of the new Child Support Package, each child will receive $2,000
of Child Credits per year up to age 16. As child-raising costs and needs
differ between families, the Child Credits are given in cash to provide
parents with maximum flexibility. Parents can opt to spend the Credits
on developmental experiences, if they desire to do so.</p>
